Source and independence

Slopless is an independently maintained derivative of Hallmark, originally by Nutlope and contributors. Our starting snapshot was de4999e0cd7a7a41df0b4e6903fa02fe9f1492a8.

The original MIT notice remains intact. Slopless adds its own identity, website, integration changes, migration tools, and verification. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original authors or Together AI.

The catalog and photography

The included themes, design references, example projects, and catalog screenshots originate in the upstream project. They demonstrate possibilities; they are not Slopless customers, client work, or evidence of new benchmark results. The evaluation tooling retains a clear provenance note.

The photo library lists images hosted on Wikimedia Commons. Each photograph keeps its own license: public domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA as recorded. MIT does not override those terms. Keep each image's required credit and check its source before redistribution. Existing photo credits remain in the example pages.

Typefaces

The main website uses locally served Manrope and IBM Plex Mono. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; the complete notices are included alongside their font files.

  • Manrope font license
  • IBM Plex Mono font license

Catalog demonstrations use their own fonts, sometimes loaded from Google Fonts or Fontshare. Those services and typefaces retain their own terms. Slopless does not claim ownership of third-party brands used to identify supported tools.
